The Longevity Score is a projected bonus in years added to your baseline life expectancy based on the healthy habits you complete today. It is not a medical prediction — it is a motivational estimate based on well-established research into lifestyle and longevity.

Each habit carries a weighted contribution: exercise, meditation, medicine adherence, hydration, and fasting each add a different amount. Missing key habits can apply small deductions. The score updates live throughout your day as you log activity.

Troubleshooting

Step-by-step fixes for the most common issues.

🔔 Notifications not working

  1. Open device Settings → Apps → LifeCompanion
  2. Tap Notifications and enable "Allow notifications"
  3. On Android: go to Battery → disable battery optimisation for the app
  4. Ensure Do Not Disturb is not blocking the app
  5. Re-add the medicine entry inside the app to reschedule the notification

🌤️ Weather not showing

  1. Check that location permission is granted: Settings → LifeCompanion → Location → "While Using"
  2. Ensure you have an active internet connection
  3. Pull down to refresh the home screen
  4. If the issue persists, disable and re-enable weather in the app settings

📉 Longevity score not updating

  1. Ensure habits are being logged via the correct tiles (not just viewed)
  2. The score updates within a few seconds of each log — wait a moment
  3. Force-close the app and reopen it
  4. Check that your date of birth is entered correctly in your profile

🎵 No sound in exercise session

  1. Check your device's volume is not muted (including media volume separately)
  2. Ensure the music toggle in the session's top-right corner is enabled
  3. Check that the audio file exercise_music.mp3 is present in the app's asset bundle — it may not be included in all builds
  4. Restart the session

🌅 Morning routine not advancing

  1. Ensure the app is in the foreground — background timers may be throttled by the OS
  2. Check that battery optimisation is not killing the app mid-session
  3. If the timer appears frozen, tap Pause then Resume to reset the tick
  4. Force-close and reopen the session

📱 App crashing on launch

  1. Force-close the app and reopen it
  2. Ensure your OS is up to date (iOS 14+ / Android 8.0+ required)
  3. Uninstall and reinstall the app (note: this will delete all local data)
  4. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and OS version

🗑️ Deleting your data

Because LifeCompanion stores all data locally on your device and requires no account, you are always in full control of your data. There is nothing stored on our servers to delete.

To delete all data immediately

  1. Uninstall LifeCompanion from your device.
  2. All habits, logs, health records, and settings are permanently deleted from your device.
  3. If you use iCloud or Google backup, also remove the app's backup data from your cloud storage settings.

To delete specific entries without uninstalling

  1. For timeline events: tap the dot on the timeline, then tap the delete icon in the popup.
  2. For habit logs: navigate to Reports → select the habit → delete individual entries.
  3. For custom habits: go to the habit settings and tap "Delete habit" to remove it and all its history.
  4. For your profile: go to Profile settings and use "Reset profile" to clear personal details.

If you have any concerns about your data or require assistance with deletion, contact us at privacy@selos.chat and we will respond within 5 business days.
