02 Common questions

Is Selos free?
Yes. All guided meditations, the meditation timer, fasting, medicine reminders and the daily workout are free. Selos Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks the training programs (Autogenic Training, Autogenic Training II and the wave program).

How do I cancel Selos Pro?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Selos. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Selos. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Open the Pro screen in Selos and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

I'm not getting reminders.
Reminders are off until you switch them on in the Ritual tab. Also check that notifications for Selos are allowed in your phone's settings. On Android, turning off battery optimisation for Selos helps reminders arrive on time.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. Selos has no accounts and no servers holding your data. Uninstalling the app deletes it. See our Privacy Policy.
