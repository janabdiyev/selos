Privacy Policy

Selos is a meditation and mind-training app. This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and what we never do with it.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The short version: Everything you log in Selos stays on your device. We have no accounts, no servers holding your data, no ads and no analytics. The only data that leaves your phone is what Apple or Google and our subscription provider need to process a Selos Pro purchase.

01 Who we are

Selos is a mobile app for meditation, Autogenic Training and related mind-training programs, with simple tools for fasting, medicine reminders and a light daily workout. "We", "us" and "our" refer to the developer of Selos, Can Abdiyev. "You" refers to the person using the app.

This policy applies to the Selos app on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

02 What the app stores

Selos only stores what you enter or what the app needs to work:

DataPurposeStorage
Practice historyMeditation, training-program progress, workouts and fasting sessions, to show streaks and insightsOn device only
Medicine names and schedulesMedicine reminders and your intake logOn device only
Reminder settingsSending the reminders you switched on, at the times you choseOn device only
App preferencesRemembering your choices, such as workout modeOn device only
Subscription statusUnlocking Selos Pro if you subscribeApple / Google and RevenueCat

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or browsing history. Selos has no sign-up and no user accounts.

03 Where it is stored

Your practice data is kept in a private database inside the app on your device. Other apps cannot read it, and Selos does not upload it anywhere. We do not operate any server that holds your data.

If you use iCloud backup (iOS) or Google backup (Android), your phone's own backup may include the app's data. That backup is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not ours.

04 Subscriptions and third-party services

Meditations in Selos are free. The training programs are part of Selos Pro, an optional subscription.

  • Apple App Store / Google Play handle all payments. We never see your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) checks whether you have an active subscription. To do this it receives a random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app and your purchase receipt from Apple or Google. It does not receive any of your practice or medicine data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Selos contains no advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking tools, and no social logins. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

05 Your rights and controls

  • See your data — everything you log is visible in the app.
  • Delete your data — you can delete individual entries in the app. Uninstalling Selos permanently deletes all its data from your device.
  • Notifications — all reminders are off until you switch them on, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Subscription data — to ask about or delete the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat, email us at the address below.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, these controls cover your rights of access, erasure and restriction. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled directly on your device.

06 Children's privacy

Selos is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

07 Data retention

Your data stays on your device for as long as Selos is installed and is deleted when you uninstall it. We keep no copies. Subscription records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for as long as they need them to provide and account for your subscription.

08 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
