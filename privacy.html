Privacy Policy

Your health data is deeply personal. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and — most importantly — what we never do with it.

Last updated: January 2025

The short version: LifeCompanion stores everything on your device. We do not have servers that hold your health data. We do not sell, share, or analyse your personal information. The only external calls the app makes are to fetch local weather (no personal data attached) and to calculate prayer times by region.

01 Who we are

LifeCompanion is a mobile application designed to help individuals track their health habits, wellbeing practices, and daily routines. References to "we", "us", or "our" refer to the LifeCompanion development team. References to "you" or "your" refer to the individual using the application.

This Privacy Policy applies to the LifeCompanion mobile application available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

02 What data we collect and why

LifeCompanion collects the minimum information necessary to provide its features. All health-related data you enter is stored exclusively on your device.

Data type Purpose Storage
Date of birth Calculate your current age and longevity score On device only
Health habits & logs Track exercise, meditation, medicine, meals, water, fasting, prayer On device only
Medication names & schedules Power medicine reminders and timeline entries On device only
Wake / sleep times Display your daily timeline accurately On device only
Approximate location Fetch local weather conditions and calculate regional prayer times Sent to weather API
Notification preferences Deliver medicine and habit reminders at the right time On device only
Faith / tradition preference Display relevant prayer times and practices On device only

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, payment information, contacts, photos, browsing history, or any identifier that links data to you personally.

03 How data is stored

All personal and health data entered into LifeCompanion is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This database is private to the application — other apps cannot access it, and it is not uploaded to any cloud service or external server by LifeCompanion.

If you use iCloud backup (iOS) or Google backup (Android), your device's built-in backup service may include the app's local database in its backup. This backup is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy respectively, not ours. You can exclude the app from device backups in your phone's settings.

We do not operate any servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure that holds your data.

04 Third-party services

LifeCompanion uses one external service:

  • Weather API — To display local weather conditions on your daily timeline, the app sends your approximate location (latitude and longitude) to a weather data provider. This call contains no personal identifiers. The weather provider's own privacy policy governs any data they retain. You can disable weather display in the app settings, which stops all location-based network calls.

We do not use:

  • Advertising networks or ad tracking SDKs
  • Analytics platforms (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude)
  • Crash reporting services that transmit personal data
  • Social login providers
  • Any form of behavioural tracking

LifeCompanion contains no advertisements. We do not allow third-party advertisers to access any information about you.

05 Your rights and controls

Because all your data lives on your device, you have full control at all times:

  • Access your data — everything you've logged is visible and browsable within the app.
  • Delete your data — uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device permanently. Individual entries can also be deleted from within the app.
  • Location permission — you can revoke location access at any time in your device settings. The app continues to function without it; weather and prayer-time features will be unavailable.
  • Notifications — you can disable all notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Data portability — we plan to add a CSV export feature in a future update so you can take your logs to any other service.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or other regions with data protection regulations, the rights above align with the access, erasure, and restriction rights provided under those frameworks. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, most requests are satisfied by managing your own device.

06 Children's privacy

LifeCompanion is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under these ages. The app is designed for adults who wish to manage their personal health habits.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has entered personal data into the app, you can remove all data by uninstalling the application from the device.

07 Data retention

Your data is retained on your device for as long as the application is installed. When you uninstall LifeCompanion, all locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device. We retain no copies of this data.

Weather API responses are not cached beyond the current session and are not stored persistently.

08 Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be communicated through an in-app notice on first launch after the update.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
